## Summary
This talk reviews how modern authentication depends on strong encryption, from protecting credentials in transit to limiting the damage of leaked tokens. It walks through a realistic vulnerability report and a threat model, then lays out practical hardening steps and a checklist teams can apply immediately.

## Key takeaways
- Transport encryption and short token lifetimes reduce the impact of credential and token leaks.
- Threat modelling helps identify gaps such as session replay before they reach production.
- Rotate keys with a documented owner, use constant-time comparison, and log authentication failures without leaking secrets.
- Verify that old sessions are revoked after a password reset, and test encryption settings in staging before deploying.
